AI Forensics
AI did
something weird.

AI systems leave evidence too. Prompts, outputs, retrieval activity, APIs, tools, agents and connected systems can all become part of the story.

The question isn't just “What did the AI say?”
It's “What happened around it, what influenced it, and what evidence supports that?”

THE AI WORKFLOW

A user interacts with an application. The application sends an instruction. The model processes it. Data may be retrieved. An API may be called. A tool may execute. An agent may continue the workflow. An output appears.

Our job is to examine the available evidence across that chain and reconstruct the relevant activity.

Prompts & instructions

Review relevant instructions and interaction context surrounding an AI event.

Retrieval & data

Examine available evidence showing what information or data sources were involved.

Outputs & behaviour

Analyse generated outputs and surrounding system activity without treating the output in isolation.

APIs, tools & agents

Trace relevant execution paths, tool activity and connected workflow records where available.
