How we started

From one stockpot to a room full of steam.

Sakura Ramen began with a simple question: what if a bowl could feel both deeply traditional and completely at home here? We studied the discipline behind Japanese ramen — broth, tare, aroma, noodle texture and timing — then built our own kitchen around it.

Today, every service starts long before the doors open. Broths are layered slowly, noodles are cooked to order, and toppings are finished moments before they reach the bowl.

“Good ramen isn't rushed. The clock is part of the recipe.”

— Sakura kitchen note, 2018

The craft

Every bowl has a sequence.

Our kitchen is built around repeatable rituals — so the experience stays generous, precise and unmistakably Sakura.

01

Stock

We build a layered broth from bones, aromatics, kombu and dried seafood, extracting depth over hours.

02

Tare

Soy, miso or salt-based seasoning concentrates the bowl's character before broth ever meets the noodles.

03

Noodles

Fresh noodles are cooked to order for the exact spring and bite each bowl needs.

04

Assembly

Broth, noodles, protein, vegetables and aroma oil come together in seconds — then straight to you.
